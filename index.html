Full screen

Voidflare is played full screen, so the whole phone is playfield.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the playfield to fly, right to every edge. The ship rides just above your finger so you can always see it. Your guns fire by themselves — there is no fire button, and there is nothing to tap.

  • Weapon — one more tier on the main gun, up to six
  • Missile — unlocks and rotates your secondary module
  • Shield — absorbs one hit
  • Rapid — faster cadence for a while
  • x2 — double score while it lasts
  • Magnet — pickups come to you
  • Bomb — clears the screen; tap the corner button or press Space

Your hitbox is the cockpit, not the wingspan — far smaller than the ship looks. Weave through the lanes rather than going around them.

Collect Side Barrage for one burst from both screen edges, or Nova Bomb for an instant blast that clears enemy bullets. Both fire once on collection and do not consume your stored bombs.

Losing a life resets all weapon upgrades and rapid fire, so the pickups are worth chasing. A red glow around an enemy means a dense attack is about to land.

Desktop: move the mouse or use WASD / arrow keys. Esc or P pauses.
