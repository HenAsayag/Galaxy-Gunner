# Reference Video Analysis

Source: user-supplied ~20.9 second portrait gameplay clip, 450×850, 30 FPS.

## Observed gameplay
- Portrait vertical shooter.
- Player ship remains primarily near lower center but can move horizontally and vertically.
- Continuous automatic forward fire.
- Enemies stream in from upper edges and sides rather than waiting in static grids.
- Several enemies follow smooth curved trails / spline-like paths.
- Enemy projectiles include orange/red lanes, blue dotted streams, green projectile fields and curved missiles.
- Homing/curving projectiles leave bright white trails.
- Explosions are extremely frequent, mostly bright yellow/orange with short lifetime.
- Combat density increases during the clip rather than resetting between visible micro-waves.
- Floating collectibles/upgrades appear inside active combat.
- `WEAPON UPGRADE` appears as a temporary centered/upper-middle message while gameplay continues.
- Score is large, white and near the upper center.
- UI is intentionally sparse so most of the portrait screen remains playable.
- Background is subdued/grey-green space/terrain so neon bullets remain highly visible.

## Animation priorities
1. Curved enemy entry paths.
2. Smooth, finger-responsive player motion.
3. Projectile trails.
4. Immediate explosion feedback.
5. Upgrade pickup glow and message animation.
6. Dense but readable bullet patterns.
7. No hard pauses between small groups.
